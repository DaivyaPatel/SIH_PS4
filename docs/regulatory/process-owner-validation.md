## Source and applicability review

- [ ] Obtain the complete official handbook, current addenda, corrigenda, annual notices/calendars and applicable orders; verify authenticity/version and any later revisions.
- [ ] Confirm whether the government-hosted candidate addendum matches the applicable issued version; resolve proposed-modification labels and unavailable Updated endpoint.
- [ ] Establish effective dates, academic year/category/institution scope and clause-level supersession. Resolve conflicts without an LLM choosing the legal interpretation.
- [ ] Check both the visible PDF columns and extracted text. Confirm printed and actual PDF page references.
- [ ] Revalidate older-source assumptions about committee constitution, visit mode, renewal period, signatures and correction/appeal rights.

## Routes and competent actions

- [ ] Approve category-specific scrutiny/EVC routes and preliminary prerequisites; add a policy-clarification path for unmapped or conflicting cases.
- [ ] Keep task completion, recommendation and competent final decision separate; approve the actual actor/authority and required evidence for each outcome.
- [ ] Approve correction scope, deficiency/re-review loops, partial responses, return/revert edges and mandatory reasons; preserve iterations and evidence versions.
- [ ] Approve separate conditional SHC and SAC triggers, mandates and appeal/window rules. Do not require an appeal for every case.
- [ ] Approve negative outcomes, holds, withdrawal, transfer, reopening and closure; post-approval obligations must survive processing closure.
- [ ] Approve committee formation/selection method, composition, exclusions, mode, scheduling authority and calendar source; optimiser cannot override mandated selection.

## Clocks, conditions and documents

- [ ] Confirm every due date's source, anchor, authoritative timestamp, cutoff, date inclusivity, time zone and working/calendar-day basis.
- [ ] Approve holiday calendar, pause eligibility, extensions, transfer effects and original/current-deadline reporting. Synthetic offsets are not official SLAs.
- [ ] Confirm condition granularity, originating order passage, evidence checklist and assigned verifier. Ambiguous dates become clarification tasks.
- [ ] Separate submitted, under review and accepted evidence; define rejection/resubmission and the exact reviewed document version.
- [ ] Resolve lease expiry, remaining validity, equality interpretation, applicable future horizon and campus/category scope against amended rules; no-expiry and unknown values need explicit handling.
- [ ] Approve renewal acceptance/replacement effects and dependent re-evaluation; unverified upload must not erase existing expiry findings.

## Integration, alerts and records

- [ ] Approve host field ownership, supported contracts and delegated action allowlist; verify source versions, stale-state rejection, deduplication, receipts and unknown-outcome reconciliation.
- [ ] Approve recipients, escalation hierarchy, reminder offsets/repeats, template/channel rules and resolution conditions; separate institution delay from reviewer/committee/integration delay.
- [ ] Require dispatch-time status/freshness checks and separate provider acceptance, delivery and read evidence. Delivery failure is not institutional non-compliance.
- [ ] Verify role/jurisdiction/assignment enforcement for actions, evidence, aggregates, reports and exports; technical operators must not gain regulatory mandate.
- [ ] Approve audit coverage, record-specific retention/legal holds/disposal, privacy/security applicability, incident process and all deployment/data paths before real data.

## Evidence and publication gate

- [ ] Reviewed expected fixtures cover each route/branch/negative outcome, unknown mapping, role/evidence/reason guard and clock boundary.
- [ ] Backend integration tests prove the fixture semantics through its actual engine/commands; offline reference-check success alone is insufficient.
- [ ] Source replay/out-of-order/version-conflict, unknown-writeback, timer restart, stale reminder, evidence replacement and unauthorised access tests pass.
- [ ] Record reviewer comments/disposition, approved source/rule versions, approver identity/mandate, scope and date; preserve author/publisher separation.
- [ ] For existing cases, approve impact preview/migration or retained versions explicitly; never rewrite a historical decision silently.
- [ ] Activate only the reviewed scope after real connector, data, hosting and operations gates. Expired deadlines trigger authorised follow-up, not autonomous statutory decisions or sanctions.
